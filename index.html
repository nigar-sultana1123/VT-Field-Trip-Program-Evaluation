How to read this dashboard

Cumulative means aggregated across all four field trips conducted between 13 March and 29 April 2026. Counts are pulled live from the Field_trip_Sessions dimension and the Student_Response_Master fact table via DAX measures (Total Sessions, Total Students Attended, % Positive – [Construct], etc.).

% Positive is the share of Likert responses landing in the top tier of the scale: "A lot," "Some," "Yes," "Yes, a lot," "Exciting," and "Interesting." A dash (—) indicates the construct was not asked at that session. Wording differs by grade band; constructs are unified through the Question Alias map.

Caveat on confidence. Self-reported confidence after a 3-hour session captures situational confidence, not durable STEM identity. Treat the 89.8% confidence figure as a positive in-the-moment signal — not evidence of long-term self-efficacy gains.

Source · FT_StudentSurvey_Response_Master · n = 619 responses VT IC · K-12 Programs · 2026
